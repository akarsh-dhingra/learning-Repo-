Deliverables

  1. Design doc (1–2 pages) in build/capstone/DESIGN.md: message protocol table (type, direction, durable/ephemeral, ack?), Pattern A or B and why, fan-out math for your biggest room, failure table (node dies, Redis dies, client on a bad network, deploy), and what you'd monitor.
  2. Implementation: multi-node, with /metrics, graceful drain, and a reconnecting client.
  3. Black-box tests written by you, using labs/_shared/test-client.ts, covering at least: auth rejection, one durable flow end-to-end across two nodes, resume or resync after a forced gap, and a slow consumer.
  4. Load test: adapt labs/0012-operating-it/loadtest.ts. Report p50/p99 of your critical path at two load levels, and explain the bottleneck you found, with evidence (metrics, CLIENT LIST, CPU).

How it's reviewed

Paste the path to DESIGN.md, your test output and your load-test numbers in the terminal. I'll review it like a senior engineer in a design review, with follow-up questions such as “what happens to an order placed during the 3 s your gateway's Redis connection was down?” Then post the design doc to r/ExperiencedDevs or r/gamedev for critique from people who run these systems.
