Redis, from cache to locks

Ten lessons following the video section by section, each 20–25 minutes, plus one dedicated assignment on distributed locking.

Goal (from MISSION.md): explain Redis confidently in system-design interviews and use it in your own Node backends. Every lesson ends with an interview rep and a hands-on assignment.

How to study each lesson fast and well: read the knowledge sections once, then do the quizzes and recall prompts without scrolling back up, then do the assignment. Paste assignment answers to Claude in the terminal for review. Come back to the quizzes two days later. Remembering something after a gap is what makes it stick.
